## en

Hello,

Thank you for buying the lead tracker. The quickest way to start:

1. Google Sheets: open sheets.google.com, then File → Import → Upload, choose the .xlsx file and
   pick "Replace spreadsheet". In Excel, just double-click the file.
2. On the Leads sheet, add one row per enquiry. Fill at least Date added, Name and Status, and
   pick Source, Status and the other drop-downs from the list.
3. Put a Next follow-up date on every lead you are still working on.
4. Do not type in the grey columns. They fill themselves: Follow-up, the Call today list and
   the Summary.
