## en

Hello,

Thank you for buying the commission ledger. The quickest way to start:

1. Google Sheets: open sheets.google.com, then File → Import → Upload, choose the .xlsx file and
   pick "Replace spreadsheet". In Excel, just double-click the file.
2. On the Deals sheet, add one row per deal with a short Deal ID such as D-001, the deal value
   and either a commission % or a fixed commission amount.
3. When money comes in, add a row on the Payments sheet with the same Deal ID. Received, Pending
   and Payment status update on their own.
4. Do not type in the grey columns. They hold the formulas.

The sheet only does the arithmetic. It is not tax advice: please ask your CA which taxes (GST,
TDS) apply to you.

The full guide came with your download, and it is also on this page:
https://tools.changelense.in/templates/commission-ledger/
