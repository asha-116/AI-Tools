## en

Hello,

Thank you for writing to us. Each template comes with a short guide in the download. The quickest
way to start with any of them:

1. Google Sheets: open sheets.google.com, then File → Import → Upload, choose the .xlsx file and
   pick "Replace spreadsheet". In Excel, just double-click the file.
2. Fill the white columns. Do not type in the grey columns: they hold the formulas and fill
   themselves.
3. Pick values from the drop-down lists where there is one, so the Summary stays correct.

Guides:

- Lead tracker: https://tools.changelense.in/templates/lead-tracker/
- Commission ledger: https://tools.changelense.in/templates/commission-ledger/

If you lost your file, get it again on https://tools.changelense.in/get-download/ (Payment ID:
{payment_id}). If your question is still open, reply and tell us which template and which sheet
you mean.

— ChangeLens support
