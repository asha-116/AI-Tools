# Brand voice — DRAFT (needs-owner)

- **Who we talk to:** busy real-estate agents in India, often on mobile, often bilingual.
- **Tone:** practical, plain, respectful. Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives.
- **Hindi:** everyday Hindi (not overly Sanskritised); keep common English terms such as EMI,
  loan, registration, PDF where agents actually use them.
- **Never:** income promises, "guaranteed", "official", fear-based urgency, legal/tax advice.
- **Always:** show how a number was calculated; link to the tool.
