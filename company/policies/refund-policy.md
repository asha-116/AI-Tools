# Refund policy — DRAFT (needs-owner)

> Status: **draft, not approved.** The public version goes in `legal/refund.md` (Phase 3)
> after owner approval. Numbers below are proposals.

- Digital products are delivered instantly. Refunds are offered when:
  1. the download link does not work and we cannot fix it within 48 hours; or
  2. the buyer was charged twice for the same order; or
  3. the buyer requests a refund within **7 days** of purchase for any reason, once per buyer.
- **Agent threshold:** the Support agent may approve refunds up to **₹0** (i.e. none) until
  Phase 5 is proven; proposed later value: **≤ ₹299**. Everything above goes to the owner.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method via Razorpay.
