1. **Changed your mind:** ask within **7 days** of purchase, for any reason. This is allowed

2. **Download does not work:** the link or file does not work and we cannot fix it within

3. **Charged twice:** you were charged more than once for the same order. We refund the extra
   payment.

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with:

- your **payment ID** (from the Razorpay receipt), and
- the reason (one line is enough).

Please do not send card or bank details — we never need them.

## How you get the money back

- The refund goes back to the **original payment method** (card, UPI, net banking, wallet) through

- Your bank or UPI app may take **[5–7] working days** more to show it _(proposal; depends on
