## 3. Payments

Payments are handled by **Razorpay**, a payment processor. Your name, email, phone number and card
/ UPI / bank details go to Razorpay, not to us. Razorpay uses them under its own privacy policy and
sends you its own receipt. Please read Razorpay's policy on its website.

## 4. Where the data is stored

Our website and its small database run on **Cloudflare**. Cloudflare's servers may be outside
India. Payment data is held by Razorpay. We do not copy your personal data anywhere else.

## 5. No selling, no AI on your personal data

- We **do not sell or rent** your personal data to anyone.
- We use some automated (AI) tools to help run the business. Before any text goes to an AI tool, we
  remove names, emails, phone numbers, addresses and payment IDs. Your personal data is **not** sent
  to AI tools.
- We do not use your data for anything other than the purposes above.

## 7. Your rights (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

You can:

1. **Access** — ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
2. **Correct or update** — ask us to fix data that is wrong or incomplete.
3. **Erase** — ask us to delete your data. We will, unless a law requires us to keep it (for
   example, order records for tax).
4. **Withdraw consent** — as easily as you gave it. Email us to leave the waitlist and we remove
   you. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.
5. **Grievance redressal** — complain to our Grievance Officer (below). If you are not satisfied,
   you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
6. **Nominate** — name another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act.

**How:** email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you used (for orders, include your payment ID).
We may ask for a few details to confirm it is you. We aim to reply within [2] working days
