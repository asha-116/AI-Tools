# Home loan balance transfer calculator

Agents often get queries about switching a home loan to a lower rate. This calculator shows, on the spot, whether the lower rate offsets the switching costs. You can plug in the current loan outstanding ₹39,39,000, current rate 8%, years left 15 years, new rate 7% and any fees ₹25,000 to see the new EMI ₹35,405, interest on the new loan ₹24,33,872, interest saved ₹4,02,892 and net saving after costs ₹3,77,892.

## How to use the calculator

1. Open the balance‑transfer page on your phone.
2. Enter the loan amount (₹39,39,000) and the remaining tenure (15 years).
3. Fill the current interest rate (8%) and the offered new rate (7%).
4. Add all switching fees you have been quoted (₹25,000).
5. Tap ‘Calculate’ – the tool instantly shows the new EMI, total interest and net saving.

## How it is calculated

- Net saving = (current EMI × months left − outstanding) − (new EMI × new months − outstanding) − switching costs
- EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan outstanding, r = yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100 and n is the number of months. Interest = EMI × months − P.

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n‑1)

- P is the loan outstanding (₹39,39,000).
- r is the monthly rate: yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100.
- n is the total number of months left.
  Net saving = (current EMI × months left – ₹39,39,000) – (new EMI × new months – ₹39,39,000) – ₹25,000.
  The first part is interest you would pay if you stay, the second part is interest after switching, and the last part subtracts the fees.

### Assumptions

- Both rates stay the same for the whole tenure. Floating rates change with the lender’s benchmark.
- Switching costs are what you enter. Ask both lenders for every charge in writing: processing fee, legal and valuation fees, stamp duty on the new mortgage, and any foreclosure charge.
- RBI does not allow foreclosure charges on floating-rate home loans to individual borrowers (RBI circular of 2 August 2019, RBI/2019-20/29). Fixed-rate loans can have them.
- Before income tax, and money saved later counts the same as money today.
- This shows numbers only. It is not advice on whether to switch. Check the offer with the lender.
