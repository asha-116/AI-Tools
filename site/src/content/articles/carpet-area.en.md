## Frequently asked questions

### What is loading and why does it matter?

Loading shows how much extra space is included in the super built‑up area compared to the carpet area. A higher loading means the buyer pays for common areas that are not usable.

### How is price per carpet sq ft calculated?

The tool divides the total price by the carpet area to give ₹17,143. This lets you compare flats on the same usable size.

### Does the calculator include parking or GST?

No. It uses only the area × rate. Additional charges like parking, GST, stamp duty are extra and should be added separately.
